# Learning with Stepstone

For interface 119 / server 41. Your mentor can help you get started. Home, Learn, Rewards and Mentor are the main menus. **Edit profile** opens your Pip, room, colours and reading settings.

## Start and return

Open the private start link from your mentor. Choose a Pip and give it a name. A new name can have up to 12 characters. Older saved names stay as they are when unchanged.

Try the starting questions: six in each of Money & math, Reading & writing, Science and Life skills, 24 in total. You may also try the optional writing. Digital and Work come after the core courses. These questions suggest a starting point; they do not decide your ability or pass modules. An older unfinished starting check keeps its original questions and version.

Follow the guided tour and finish with **Save my goal & continue**. The starting questions and tour each give five welcome Steps once. On Home, **Your starting plan → Start from my plan** helps you choose where to begin. A returning learner can use **Continue where I left off**.

Keep your private return link or sign-in code safe. If you lose it, ask your mentor for a return link to your existing account. Do not start another learner account just to return.

## Learn one module at a time

1. Read or listen to the worked example. Replay it when useful.
2. Try the activity and practice. **Show a helpful step** points out common pitfalls.
3. Review the skill, then take its six-question check.
4. Answer all six, explain one part of your thinking and say what support you used. You may draw your working.
5. Read the result and any mentor feedback in **Rewards → My wins**.

Five correct answers out of six passes. You can use supports and take the time you need. A pass is learning evidence; it is not an official qualification. Your explanation and practical work may need a conversation with your mentor.

The saved module check uses situations separate from the published practice. A later attempt may repeat check questions you have already seen. Use the explanations to learn, and try a changed example with your mentor when checking what you can do independently.

Worked examples, activities and questions share one lesson flow. An eight-second progress bar follows a correct practice answer; **Stop** lets you pause it. A saved answered question waits for **Next** when reopened. Your work saves as you go. If you see a message saying a report is waiting to send, reconnect and send it before switching learners.

## Your courses

The suggested order is Money & math, Reading & writing, Science, Life skills, Digital skills, then Work & jobs. Each has 24 modules. Complete all 24 in each of the first four courses to unlock Digital and Work: 96 named modules. The app shows what to pass next.

At the same milestone you can ask for a starter laptop when your mentor has assigned and funded one. The mentor checks the agreed demonstrations and arranges the handoff. No Steps are spent for a new laptop offer. Older saved reward agreements keep their own recorded terms.

## Today's tasks and your Pip

Home shows three daily tasks: open Stepstone, complete a module, and play with your named Pip. A review can count as completing today's module task.

Your Pip has its own greeting and animations. You can greet it while its food bowl is empty. Feed it to play and use its toys. A first pass in a new module earns a food portion. Some later modules can earn a replay portion every 24 hours. A portion gives 30 minutes of play energy; the bowl holds up to three portions. Food and Steps use different rules.

In **Edit profile**, choose any of the 28 Pips and any of the 16 interface colours. Colours are free. Accessories and room items unlock as you complete modules. Your room can have colours, rugs, furniture, decorations, paintings, shelves, a food bowl and curtains. Drag an item to move it. To remove it, drag it into the bin at the back right of the room; its lid opens while you move an item. Removing an item keeps it available in your collection.

**Quiet visuals** reduces movement. Choose a reading voice, use **Try voice**, or press **Stop reading**. Soft voice depends on the host's voice setup. Reading and drawing support do not reduce your grade.

## Steps, rewards and support

A first finished module check gives five Steps. Its first pass gives 15 more. A passing review can give five Steps after seven days. Fast repeats do not award those Steps again. Practice before connecting to your mentor can stay on this device, but it does not award connected lesson Steps or send reports.

Your mentor must fund real rewards before they are available. Gift-card choices are CAD10 for 20 Steps, CAD20 for 36 Steps or CAD50 for 80 Steps, with other saved skill and program requirements. A request is not a promise of immediate delivery. Rewards shows its status. Laptop and AI support also need their own funding and mentor review.

In **Mentor → Support requests**, choose a preset request. Your mentor can send a preset reply. This is not live chat. Your mentor sees your completed checks, explanations, drawings and support choices; other learners cannot see them.

## Shared devices and saving

Use the account and sign-in controls in your profile to **Save & switch learner**. If a draft or report is waiting, follow that message and save it first. Keep private codes away from other people using the device. A separate local installation keeps separate records.

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A menu will not open during setup | Follow the current tour arrow |
| Your report is waiting to send | Reconnect, then choose Try sending now in the lesson or Try now in My wins |
| This check can no longer be sent | Choose Start a fresh check |
| Too many sign-in attempts | Wait 15 minutes |
| A name is refused | Try another name or ask your mentor |
| Support requests is locked | Connect to your mentor first |
| Something else is wrong | Tell your mentor what the screen says |

Use made-up details in practice. Do not enter real passwords, ID numbers or bank numbers into a lesson. This guide does not replace the official instructions for real applications or services.
