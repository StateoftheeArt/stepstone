# Pip and mentor support

Current behaviour: interface 119 / server 41.

Pip is the learner’s own companion, chosen and named during setup. The entry
mascots are Learner Pip (fox) and Mentor Pip (owl). After profile creation, the
learner's named companion follows the guided-tour dialogue. All 28 companions
are available from the beginning. Profile editing and pet interaction use
separate controls.

## Included

- After age, the Welcome page asks for a name, companion and companion name.
  The starting-check introduction follows profile creation. New Pip names have a
  12-character limit; an existing longer name can stay when editing another option.
- **Edit profile** includes Pip, Pip's home, accessories, colours and Reading &
  comfort. There are 16 free colours, 26 accessories and eight room categories.
  Selected colour changes preview in the app and roll back on cancel.
- Tap to cycle through the creature's three dances. The thirteenth tap produces
  an inflation, puff and brief annoyed expression, then returns to idle. Holding
  does not trigger a pop. Dragging does not count as a tap.
  Quiet visuals and reduced motion use still reactions.
- Drag Pip to a clear place on screen. Only the pet moves: its profile slot and edit
  control retain their position and size. Positions are saved separately for each
  learner, constrained to the viewport, and moved clear of answer controls.
  A centered magnet occupies the empty pet slot. Drop Pip onto it or tap it to put
  Pip away. Arrow keys move Pip; Home or Return companion to my profile also returns it.
  During setup, the same pet follows the compact dialogue without overwriting a
  saved screen position. Parking or dragging pauses that placement for the current step.
  The next step brings Pip to its next dialogue. There is no duplicate full-width banner.
- Profile names shorten visually when space is tight; full values remain in the
  editor. Names, creature, colour, interests, motivation, home and accessories
  belong to the current learner. Existing cosmetic values remain compatible.
- A first module pass supplies food; selected completed modules can supply another
  portion on a replay, at most once per 24 hours. One portion restores energy for
  30 minutes, with capacity for three portions. Greeting needs no food; play and
  toys use energy. Pet care and profile edits award no Steps.
- Mentor space shows a learner connection/feedback/rewards hub or a mentor’s private
  manager cards according to their role. Support requests require a connection.
  Learners choose preset requests; mentors choose preset replies. Neither gets a
  free-text chat composer. Draft selections survive reload in the same tab, and
  switching learners clears them. Retry keeps the same request identity.
- Actual written and drawn assignment work still reaches the mentor for human review.
  Preset support does not make that review channel incapable of carrying a message.

## Engagement and operating limits

Pet interactions, profile edits and support requests earn no points. The two welcome
milestones remain five points each. The companion does not choose answers.

The app remains local. Shared HTTPS hosting needs funding. Support is asynchronous,
with no push alerts or guaranteed reply time. Mentor accounts require sign-in and an
approved browser; being on the same Wi-Fi does not grant manager access.

Current pilot evidence is in [verification scope](VERIFICATION.md).
Earlier companion and header receipts describe their own builds. Responsive
browser checks do not replace an actual phone or tablet acceptance run.
