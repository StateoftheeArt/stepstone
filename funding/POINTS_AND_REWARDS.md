# Stepstone Steps and rewards

Current documented behaviour for interface 119 / server 41. Refreshed from local source; this is not a new funding commitment.
No real funds, card purchases, device handoffs or subscriptions are established by
software testing. This installation remains local until hosting is funded.

## Laptop offers in the current course structure

New laptop offers require all 24 modules in each of Money & math, Reading & writing, Science and Life skills: 96 named core modules. Digital and Work unlock at that same milestone. A purchased or donated laptop must be assigned, and the mentor reviews the five demonstrations before approval. The laptop has zero Step cost. Existing saved offers retain their recorded requirements; the older 32-skill path below remains relevant to earlier agreements and some card gates, not to the current course-unlock rule.

## Earning Steps

| Milestone | Steps |
| --- | ---: |
| Finish starting questions, once | 5 |
| Finish guided setup, once | 5 |
| First finished six-question check in a module | 5 |
| First pass in that module | 15 more |
| Passing review at least seven days after the previous pass | 5 |

Setup Steps are recognition, not an instant payout. Repeating setup does not
award them again. Fast retakes do not repeat first-pass Steps. Supported learning
is allowed. Colour choices are free. The mentor's My learning record is separate
and cannot claim funded learner rewards.

## Gift-card exchange

| Canadian value | Step cost | Saving compared with $10 cards |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $10 | 20 | — |
| $20 | 36 | 4 Steps |
| $50 | 80 | 20 Steps |

The minimum is CAD10. The lifetime cap is **CAD250 of gift cards per confirmed
person in one mentor program**. Delivered and waiting requests count. Cancellation
removes a waiting request from that total. Linked extra accounts cannot redeem
another allowance. Known earlier CAD5 claims count at their recorded denomination;
custom earlier agreements without face values need reconciliation before using
this exchange. Do not invent values. Existing recorded claims are retained.
A mentor must verify people; the app cannot identify one human across unrelated
mentor groups or prevent collusion by administrators.

Before a request, the learner must complete the mandatory setup and all named
**Everyday decisions** checks in their saved foundation path. Tutorial Steps alone,
placement answers, unfinished checks and unrelated modules cannot satisfy this gate.
The mentor confirms the main learner account and reserves real received cash.
There can be one open card request per person. A changed example must be verified
by the mentor before approval; speaking, drawing, typing and supports all count.
No automated accusation of AI use is made.

The learner chooses Amazon.ca, Walmart Canada or Google Play Canada, and one
funded amount. The server owns the price, value, cap and balance calculation.
It deducts Steps when the request is accepted. Idempotent retries and transaction
checks prevent a duplicate deduction. A cancellation refunds Steps once; it leaves
uncommitted funding reserved for the same learner until the mentor releases it.

## Funding and private code delivery

1. Record money actually received. Do not record pledges as money.
2. Confirm a learner's main account in Learners, then Set aside rewards. Reserve
   whole CAD10 amounts from available cash. The reserve cannot be spent elsewhere.
3. Review the learner's request. Confirm a changed skill example, then approve it.
4. Purchase an unused Canadian card for exactly the requested provider and value.
5. Enter its key and Walmart PIN when required. Confirm it is unused and matches
   the value, then send it privately to the learner's Rewards page.

Sending the key records the purchase once and reduces the cash reserve by the same
amount. **Do not separately record that purchase as an expense.** Approval is not
delivery. Delivery is irreversible in the app. The record establishes code delivery,
not proof that the provider accepted the card or that a learner spent it.

Only supported ASCII keys and the required numeric PIN are accepted. Links, spaces,
unsupported symbols, selected message phrases and extra delivery fields are refused.
These are format limits, not authenticity, value or complete covert-message detection.
A real key outside a supported format is refused rather than treated as a message.
The mentor checks the purchased card with the provider.

Keys are excluded from ordinary account snapshots, report exports, command results
and audit details. Only the receiving learner can explicitly reveal a delivered key
through the app. Other groups cannot use that endpoint. The private SQLite database
and its recovery copies contain the keys; protect their storage and host access.
They are not encrypted by this app. Do not place keys in receipt notes or reports.

Cash, card reserves, purchases, cancellations and actual delivery remain distinct.
After restoring a backup, reconcile real rewards before reopening requests, so a
restored old record cannot automatically issue another card. No automatic purchase,
cash transfer, external notification or payment collection happens in Stepstone.

A CAD20 initial budget per learner is an example, not a commitment to fund every
learner to the lifetime cap. Ten CAD20 budgets need CAD200 actually received,
plus separately covered fundraising fees. Reserve only what is funded. Learning,
food, care, safety and normal device access never depend on Steps or funding.
The app and rewards do not compensate the mentor. Hosting is a separate cost.

## Laptop, PC, typing and AI

Laptop and AI funding are **separate from the CAD250 card limit**. The learning
laptop has zero Step cost. New offers require the 96 named modules in the four
core courses; earlier saved offers retain their recorded path. Every offer needs
a separately secured checked laptop and charger and the five useful demonstrations:
save/find a file, communicate clearly, check a changed budget, check a scam and
check an AI answer. Phone examples and supports are allowed; learners do not need
to own a PC already to qualify.

Then follow [the PC and AI path](../PC_AND_AI_PATH.md): PC basics, useful setup,
safe AI workflow, typing practice and a personal useful app. Typing speed is not
a reward gate. One permitted monthly AI plan can be reserved up to CAD50 including
charges, separately from cards and laptops. This is a spending cap, not a provider
price or automatic subscription. Check current age, consent, account, price and
renewal rules before purchasing; never share an adult login or use a false age.
Do not begin the month until the learner is ready to build. No annual plan,
usage-based add-ons, automatic renewal or recurring spending is authorized.

## Oversight and evidence

Mentor space holds person confirmation, invitations, preset support requests,
written-work review, funding and reward decisions. Preset messages restrict casual
chat; assignments and drawings remain an intentional human-reviewed channel.
Do not claim that filtering, Steps or gift-card caps eliminate fraud or hidden
communication. Review a changed example rather than treating a score as proof.
