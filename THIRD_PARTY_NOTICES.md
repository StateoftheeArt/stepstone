## Original Canadian-dollar value diagrams

The five `public/money/cad-*-value119.svg` diagrams and `tools/build_teaching_money.py` are original Stepstone work, copyright 2026 Stepstone contributors, under the root MIT license. They use geometric shapes and denomination text, with no copied bank-note portraits, logos, engraved designs or coin artwork. They represent dollar values for arithmetic; they are not currency reproductions.

The Bank of Canada Museum is linked as an optional resource for recognising real Canadian cash: https://www.bankofcanadamuseum.ca/2020/11/printable-play-money/ . No Museum images or PDF are included in this source release. Earlier local builds used extracted Museum teaching-money images; those files are excluded from this package. The linked resource keeps its own terms and is not relicensed by Stepstone. Stepstone has no affiliation with or endorsement from the Bank of Canada or the Royal Canadian Mint.
