# Mentoring with Stepstone

For interface 119 / server 41. This installation remains local. A shared HTTPS address and acceptance on real learner devices remain separate work.

## Account and browser access

Choose **I'm mentoring → Create account**. Set your display name, username, passphrase of at least 12 characters and a name for this browser. Save the one-time private recovery key outside this browser. Learners do not need that key or your passphrase.

Ordinary registration creates a separate empty workspace. Only the installation owner on loopback can choose **Keep the existing records on this host**; the previous mentor passphrase is required when one exists. Existing records are not made available to remote registrations.

Stepstone recognises an approved browser for up to a year. A browser profile is the approved device, not an entire computer or Wi-Fi network. Clearing its data removes that recognition. On a new browser, enter your username and passphrase, name the browser, then approve the matching code from **Mentor → Account & settings** on an approved browser. Use recovery only if you cannot use one: recovery retains learner records and revokes prior browser access. Keep an extra approved browser and a private recovery copy.

Background status refreshes now preserve typed and autofilled sign-in fields. They do not save the passphrase into browser storage.

## Your dashboard

The dashboard has **Learners**, **Progress inbox**, **Support requests**, **Invitations**, **Rewards & funding**, **My test space**, and **Account & settings**. Open a card to work on that area. The build badge in settings is generated from the current release.

In **Invitations**, create a private start link for each learner. It is single-use and expires after seven days. After joining, the learner names and chooses a Pip, tries starting questions and completes the guided tour. For a returning learner use **Help them sign in** on their existing record. A rotated return code signs out earlier learner sessions; do not create duplicate accounts for recovery.

## Starting and progressing

New starting checks contain 24 questions: six each for Money & math, Reading & writing, Science and Life skills. Digital and Work are not assessed by this new starting check because they begin after the core courses. Writing is optional. Suggestions are informal and separate for each area; age does not select ability. Existing starting records keep their version and original questions, including older 48-question checks. The starting check and tour award five Steps each once, rather than passing modules.

There are 144 active modules, 24 in each course, plus nine older modules retained for saved records. The suggested order is the four core courses, then Digital, then Work. Both later courses require all 96 named core modules to be passed. Finishing Digital is recommended before Work; it is not an additional server lock. Unfinished checks and placement answers do not satisfy the course gate.

The older saved foundation path remains a separate recorded agreement. New laptop offers use the 96 core modules. Existing saved reward offers retain their requirements; adding a release does not rewrite a learner's agreement.

## Review evidence with the learner

Lessons include examples, activities, practice, review and a six-question check. Five of six passes. The app grades selected and numeric answers; it does not automatically judge the learner's written explanation, drawing, writing quality or real-device mastery.

**Progress inbox → Review** shows the six answers, explanations, the learner's reflection, their reported support and any drawing. Support and practice retries are self-reported, not verified. Ask for a changed example or a short explanation. Speaking, drawing, typing and supports can all show understanding. Save encouragement or a concrete next step to the learner's feedback.

All 153 ready module banks now have private check cases separate from published practice. A six-question draw covers six distinct situations, both lessons and the module's required skills. Some banks have exactly six questions, so later attempts can repeat cases. A started check retains its stored questions during an update. Unconnected preview checks deliberately use public practice and do not produce connected rewards or reports. Review the explanation and ask for a changed example before treating a score as independent mastery. Scores alone are insufficient evidence for high-value rewards; no proctoring or automatic AI-use accusation is provided.

## Pip, daily practice and comfort

Home shows opening Stepstone, completing a module and playing with the learner's named Pip as daily tasks. The profile has 28 companions, 26 accessories, 16 free interface colours and eight room-item categories. Accessories and home items unlock through distinct module completions. Removing a room item keeps ownership; drag it into the bin to remove it from the scene.

A first pass earns one food portion. Selected later modules also earn a replay portion every 24 hours. Each portion provides 30 minutes of play energy, with capacity three. These food rules are separate from the seven-day Step review interval. A resting Pip can greet the learner without food; play and toys need energy. Quiet visuals and reading options support comfort.

## Steps and funded rewards

Use **Rewards & funding** to record actual received money or purchased/donated stock, confirm the real person's main learner account and set aside available funding. Do not record pledges as received cash. A linked extra account does not create another reward allowance. The mentor's personal test space cannot claim funded learner rewards.

The first finished module check awards five Steps and its first pass 15 more. A passing review can award five after seven days. Setup awards and first passes do not repeat. Gift-card exchanges cost 20 Steps for CAD10, 36 for CAD20 or 80 for CAD50. The lifetime gift-card cap is CAD250 per confirmed person within one mentor program. Saved Everyday decisions requirements, a changed example and sufficient funding also apply.

Approve a request only after the required review. For a card, purchase the exact unused Canadian provider and value, enter the key and required PIN, and confirm private delivery in the app. Sending the code records the purchase once; do not also record it as an expense. Approval is not delivery. Delivered codes cannot be undone in the app. Do not put real codes into shared screenshots or test receipts.

For a new laptop offer, pass all four core courses, assign funded stock, and review demonstrations of finding a saved file, communicating clearly, adapting a budget, checking a scam and checking an AI answer. This laptop offer has zero Step cost. Older offers retain their recorded terms. Demonstrations can be supported and prepared on a phone; a learner need not already own a laptop to request one.

The local laptop comparison helper checks the configured Dell Canada and HP Canada pages for new, unrefurbished machines meeting its processor, memory and SSD criteria, then sorts verified matches by advertised price. Tax, shipping and delivery availability are excluded. Unverified listings are omitted. It does not establish today's lowest price across the market or buy anything. Verify condition, processor generation, warranty, seller, shipping and tax before purchase. See [the route](PC_AND_AI_PATH.md) and [the reward policy](funding/POINTS_AND_REWARDS.md).

An eligible funded AI month requires its own review, saved requirements and available funding, with a CAD50 spending ceiling. Check the provider's current eligibility, terms and price before purchasing; the ceiling is not a quoted subscription price.

## Privacy, recovery and test space

Each mentor manages their own private learner workspace. Support requests and replies are preset messages, not live chat, email, push notifications or an emergency service. Other learners cannot see checks or feedback. Teach with fictional details and keep private codes and real funding out of demonstrations.

Use **My test space** for personal learning. Its reset requires confirmation and affects that personal record, not learner accounts. Never simulate prerequisites or rewards in the live learner database to prove a workflow.

**Account & settings** shows on-host recovery status and can create a checked consistent copy. Follow [OPERATIONS.md](OPERATIONS.md) for restoration and reconciliation. A host restore revokes access, expires unused invites and pauses reward decisions pending reconciliation of later physical deliveries. Keep a separate secure copy; a backup on the same disk is not disaster recovery.
