Privacy and your practice.

This is a public practice demo. It has no learner accounts, private invitations, mentor passwords or shared learner records.

  • Your lesson answers, drafts, results and Pip choices are saved in this browser. Someone else using the same browser profile on this device can see them.
  • The mentor report is fictional. The feedback preview stays on the page and is never sent to a learner.
  • Stepstone adds no advertising or analytics tracker. The hosting provider receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to serve the site.
  • Read-aloud in this demo uses only voices the browser identifies as local to your device. If no local English voice is available, the text remains on screen.
  • Lesson resources and feedback links open other websites when you choose them. Those websites have their own privacy rules.
  • Use made-up details for practice. Do not enter passwords, recovery codes, addresses, financial account details or someone else's personal information.

You can revisit a lesson from My practice. Clearing this site's browser data removes saved practice; it cannot be recovered from a server.

Real learner invitations and private mentor feedback belong to a separate, connected installation of the full app.
